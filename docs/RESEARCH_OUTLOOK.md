# Research directions

DNO's current-state evolution and shared-generative architecture can be trained and compared at larger scales and on a wider variety of input environments.

Potential directions include persistent effective state across longer periods, conditional interaction and formation of reusable structures, observation that changes through actual contact while non-observation remains possible, language and action-stream computation, visual and audiovisual sequences, numerical and continuous signals, and hybrid use with Transformer, recurrent, graph and state-space architectures.

The research goal is to examine actual behavior under different datasets, compute budgets and tasks. Those experiments may reveal further computational organization, unexpected effects, different efficiency characteristics or complementary abilities. Comparisons should record their actual training and evaluation conditions rather than predefine the outcomes.

These directions do not introduce additional core algorithm categories or an independent required observation module.
